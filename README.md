# N-Body Simulation of Galaxy Collision Dynamics

Conducted a computational study of a major galactic merger between two Milky Way-analog spiral galaxies over a 1.3 billion-year timescale utilizing an N-body simulator provided by ASTRO-NOT. The project investigated the evolutionary dynamics of gravitational tidal interactions and the resulting morphological transformations.

## Analytical Dynamics
To preserve realistic disk geometry during the encounter, the collision parameters were configured with customized orbital inclinations of 48° and 62° at a 12 kpc pericenter. The galactic rotation curves were modeled using composite Plummer bulges and Hernquist dark-matter halos.

<img width="1508" height="990" alt="galaxy_collision_science (3)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b5c0771-34a4-4754-920a-26f4afa02c23" />

The study analyzed orbital decay driven by Chandrasekhar dynamical friction, quantitatively mapping key interaction phases including the first pericentric passage at approximately 200 Myr, the apocenter near 500 Myr, and the ultimate merger around 950 Myr. The comprehensive 1320-Myr evolutionary simulation captured the initial galactic approach, the transient formation of extended tidal tails, and the final coalescence into a relaxed spheroidal remnant.
